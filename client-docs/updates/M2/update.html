Milestone 2 Complete  —  Core Platform & User Roles
On track · 2 of 5 milestones

What we completed

Secure accounts & loginSign-up, login, logout, and password reset, built on Supabase as agreed on our November 12 call. Each user is guided to the right dashboard for their role.
Profiles for pharmacy professionalsPharmacists, technicians, assistants, and students each get a profile with their role, experience, location, and rate.
Pharmacy accounts with owners & managersA pharmacy signs up as its own organization. The owner manages the profile and store locations and can invite managers to help run them.
Licence & credential uploadsProfessionals upload their licence and certificates as PDFs to secure private storage. The admin approves or rejects each one with a note, and the professional sees the result.
Super admin portalOne place to see every user and pharmacy, work through the credential review queue, and manage the pharmacy chain catalog.
Pharmacy chain brandingThe admin adds chains with their logos, and each pharmacy picks its chain. This sets up the branded shift listings coming in Milestone 3.
Works on phone, tablet & desktopEvery screen adapts to smaller screens, in ShiftStarter's colours and logo.

Who can do what

Pharmacy professionals
Pharmacist · Pharmacy Technician · Pharmacy Assistant · Pharmacy Student
Pharmacy team
Owner: full control, including team members.
Manager: runs locations, but can't change the team.
Platform
Super admin: oversees all users and pharmacies, reviews credentials, and manages chains.

Tested before handover

Tested like a real userEvery feature was tested in a real browser. Issues found were fixed and retested before sign-off.
Each pharmacy's data stays privateOne pharmacy can't see or change another's details, locations, or staff, in the app or in the database.
Credential files are protectedOnly the professional who uploaded a licence and the admin can open it. Unsafe or disguised files are rejected.
One account, one roleEach email is either a professional's account or a pharmacy team member's. Someone who is both uses a second email.

Milestone 3 — Shifts, Search, Bookings & Rewards
Pharmacies post shifts, and professionals search by location and filters, apply, and get booked. Shift listings carry each pharmacy's chain branding. The rewards program follows, letting professionals earn rewards for completed shifts and redeem them as gift cards.

Why this matters

Ready for many pharmaciesEach pharmacy gets its own private space, so ShiftStarter can grow from one store to whole chains.
Trusted professionalsEvery licence is reviewed by an admin. This lets us make sure only verified professionals take shifts.
