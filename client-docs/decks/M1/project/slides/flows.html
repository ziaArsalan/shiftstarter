ShiftStarter · Milestone 1 walkthrough

07

These are the four journeys the platform is designed around. A worker registers, builds a profile, uploads credentials, searches for shifts nearby, applies, gets booked, works the shift, gets paid and leaves a review. A pharmacy registers, adds its locations, posts a shift, reviews applicants, accepts one, messages them, then pays and reviews. Credentials go through an admin check before they count as verified, and either side can raise a dispute that an admin resolves. Milestones 2 through 4 build these journeys step by step.
