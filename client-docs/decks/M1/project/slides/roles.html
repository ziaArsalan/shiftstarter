Roles & access

Three roles, each with clear limits

Worker

  • Profile & availability
  • Upload credentials
  • Search & apply to shifts
  • Bookings & messages
  • Get paid, leave reviews

Pharmacy

  • Profile & locations
  • Post & manage shifts
  • Choose applicants
  • Pay for shifts
  • Message & review workers

Admin

  • Verify credentials
  • Manage users
  • Resolve disputes
  • Platform reports

Enforced on the server for every request — not just hidden in the interface.

ShiftStarter · Milestone 1 walkthrough

05

Every account has exactly one role. Workers — pharmacists and pharmacy staff — manage their profile, upload credentials, find and apply for shifts, and get paid. Pharmacies set up their business and locations, post shifts, pick who to book, and pay. Admins verify credentials, manage users, resolve disputes and see platform reports. Importantly, these rules are checked by the server on every request, and a pharmacy can only ever touch its own shifts — so it’s secure even if someone bypasses the website.
