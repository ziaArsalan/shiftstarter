ShiftStarter · Milestone 2 walkthrough

06

These are the journeys that work today. A professional signs up, picks their role, confirms their email, builds a profile and uploads their licence, then sees the review result. A pharmacy owner signs up, names the pharmacy, picks its chain, adds store locations and invites managers. Behind the scenes, the admin reviews each licence and approves it or rejects it with a note. The admin also adds chains with their logos, which pharmacies then choose. In Milestone 3 these journeys continue into posting, finding and booking shifts.
