Milestone 3 is the heart of the marketplace. Pharmacies post and manage shifts. Professionals search for shifts nearby with filters, apply, and can propose a rate. The pharmacy books one, and the booking is confirmed. Listings carry each pharmacy’s chain branding. Then comes the rewards program: professionals earn rewards for completed shifts and redeem them as gift cards through Blackhawk. We can start the shift features right away; the gift-card part needs the Blackhawk sandbox keys.
