07

Before handing this over we tested every feature in a real browser, the way a user would. The most important checks were about privacy: in 29 out of 29 checks, one pharmacy could not see or change another pharmacy’s data, through the website or directly in the database. Testing found seven issues, mostly small usability ones, and all seven were fixed and retested. Licence files can only be opened by the professional who uploaded them and by the admin, and unsafe or disguised files are refused. And every screen was checked at phone width.
