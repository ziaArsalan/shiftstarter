Here’s what Milestone 2 delivered. Secure sign-up, login and password reset. Profiles for the four kinds of pharmacy professional. Pharmacy accounts with owners, managers and store locations. Licence and certificate uploads into private storage, reviewed by the admin. A super admin portal that shows every user and pharmacy and has the review queue. And chain branding: the admin adds chains with their logos, and each pharmacy picks its chain. Every screen also works on a phone.
