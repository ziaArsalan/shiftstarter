ShiftStarter · Milestone 2 walkthrough

05

In Milestone 1 we planned three roles. After our November 12 call that became seven. The four pharmacy professional roles: pharmacist, technician, assistant and student. The pharmacy team: an owner who controls the pharmacy profile, its chain and its team, and managers the owner invites to help run the locations. Managers can’t change the team. And the super admin, who oversees everything. Each email holds one role, so a professional who also owns a pharmacy uses a second email. Permissions are checked on every request by both the server and the database.
